## Where the tags go (no new column, nothing on the price area)
- Key Levels column: the tag sits on its level's row ("PbPOC 7600.50 [ENT Pdoc]", "G2 4180 [1/2]"). A level that is not already a row
  (the PB POC, a non-0DTE gamma level, G2...) gets a row only while its tag lives.
- Liquidity column: a tag on a book level is appended to that row's text ("50 EXIT ?").
- Colours: ENT green (long) / red (short), EXIT / 1/2 / rest amber, dark text. Plain ASCII.

## Rules (EntryBadge.h; per market from the step-2 study; only the newest drawn label counts)
- entry0 = the close of the label's decision bar; stop = pivot bar low - 1 tick (long) / high + 1 tick (short); target = the wave extreme.
- ES: ENT at the PB POC of a Pdoc -> "ENT Pdoc". NG: same with "?". CL: ENT at the gamma level between the stop and entry0 (nearest entry0)
  after Pb / Pboc / Pdoc -> "ENT ?"; after the fill "EXIT ?" on the first real book level >= 0.5R beyond the ENT price (the study rule = the
  nearest; the mockup had shown the biggest, 99.50 - the study rule gives 99.60 on that trade). GC: "1/2" on the first gamma / key level
  >= 0.5R beyond entry0, "rest" on the next one more than 0.25 ATR beyond it. NQ / HG / 6E: nothing.
- An ENT level must be beyond the stop by > 1 tick and >= 0.1 ATR from entry0.
- PULL: once price traded at the ENT level, a decided candle whose lsBookFlow defense (fact D) is "defenders leaving" (code 2 bids for a
  long / 4 offers for a short) -> " PULL", kept.
- Lifecycle on CLOSED bars: ENT from the decision bar until the stop trades, the target trades before the fill, 20 bars pass unfilled, or
  after the fill the stop / target trades. EXIT only after the fill, until it trades. GC tags until traded / stop / 40 bars.
- Not built: the X? (3 of 5 extreme signals) exit trigger - the CL EXIT uses the book level only.
