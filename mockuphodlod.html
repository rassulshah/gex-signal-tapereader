The premise, in your words: a daily candle is either OHLC or OLHC — the high comes first or the low does. If you can tell that one extreme is in, the rest of the day travels toward the other. Every column is chosen to answer that one question.

The three that carry the trade: 1st ext says an extreme has printed and when. held is the confidence — an extreme that has stood two hours is a very different thing from one printed ten minutes ago. run after 1st is the ride itself, and today's realised figure against the median tells you how much is plausibly left.

⚠ THE NUMBERS ABOVE ARE REAL AND THE SAMPLE IS TOO SMALL. They are computed from your own recorded sessions (2026-08-18 to 08-25): 5 of 6 set the high first, median first extreme 10:34, median second 13:56, median range 4.4. This section should ship counting, and start speaking only once it has enough days to mean something.
