1 · Header magnets & the King badge

★ SUP / 👑 / ★ RES badges — strongest support below, the King, strongest resistance above, each with %King strength. Note an approved divergence: the ★ picks blend strength with proximity (your 2026-08-13 decision — "more practical intraday"), while pure Academy doctrine ranks by size alone. So a huge-but-far floor can lose the ★ to a strong-and-near one. Use: the triangle SUP–KING–RES is the room price is trading in.
King badge (gold pill) — King strike + signed offset vs price (+n↑ above / −n↓ below), gatekeeper strike + its distance underneath. Dimmed gate = clear path to the King.

4 · King Path (history chart) — and how to read King MOVEMENT

The staircase — King strike vs clock time. Steps at each confirmed roll; color by running trend with hysteresis (won't recolor on 1-strike wiggles). Dashed blue = price. Gutter = price pill + 👑 label, physically outside the plot. drift chip (in-chart) = net migration + roll count — descriptive only: tested 50.0% vs next-30m (n=68), a coin flip. Verdict pill = drift+magnet+stability composite (context, not signal). pinned Xm = time at the current strike.

The movement vocabulary

One-way migration (steps keep stepping the same way)Trend evidence — dealers re-centering directionally; expect stairstepping continuation⚖ rolling-floors: 1=noise, 2=signal, 3=confirmation

Today, read with this vocabulary: crown opened at 780 ($1.40B) → bled −29% by 11am (stale-crown signature) → succession lit 775 → crown collapsed down to the 775/776 contest → afternoon flip-flopped 775↔776↔777 with 7 rolls (whipsaw core signature) while price orbited 776. 6 · THE KING READ — the 7-step process

1
Trust: no ⚠ tape, no suppression panel. If flagged — stop, nothing else counts.
2
Anchor quality: KING tile (strike + polarity) + K$ trend. Growing +γ King = strong sticky magnet. Bleeding King = discount everything downstream and expect relocation.
3
Succession scan: ⚠ hot? Then the projected King is the operative anchor for targets — the current crown is a lame duck (📊 76%, median 12 min).
4
Geometry: DIST inside gravity (≤3)? VALUE inside or outside? Inside both = rotation regime toward the King. Outside value = imbalance — do not fade, expect continuation.
5
Approach & clock: converging or receding (📊 63% vs 47%)? ETA vs PHASE: late+near = pin window; early reach = drive-off risk; LUNCH = downgrade conviction.
6
Contact: TAPS (freshness left?) and overshoot state (+γ King overshot without a 14-bar extreme = Beach-Ball reversion watch ⚖).
7
Confluence: ALIGN ✓/✗, Node Map agreement, deflections at the same strikes. Then read the projected chart as the composed conclusion of steps 2–6 — if it doesn't match your own step-through, one of the inputs changed: find which tile.
