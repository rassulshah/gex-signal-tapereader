Both resolutions arrive on the close of the 3-minute bar, never on a forming one — up to three minutes after the low prints. That latency is his rule ("lets wait for the close"), not a defect, and the panel should not pretend to be faster than its own evidence.

The wick clause is reported, not weighted. reaction has been recording rejection quality for eleven days: 49.1 % confirmed vs 48.6 % weak on 970 rows. It goes on the line because it describes what happened; it earns no emphasis, no colour of its own, and no place in any verdict until something moves that number.

What this build may not claim

  • The touch has no edge. Two independent samples now: 56 % break on 8 hand-labelled sessions, 47.9 % break on 94 episodes from the 11-day archive. Overlapping intervals, same answer.
  • Nothing survived the feature search. ~40 cells, 2 flagged — exactly the false-positive rate chance delivers. grade A holding 32.4 % is the loudest and it points backwards; it earns a pre-registered forward test, not a fix.
  • So this build makes the test visible and recorded — it does not predict the resolution. No wording on the face may suggest otherwise, and that constraint is enforced in the test suite, not left to judgement.

What the panel keeps that does have an edge is the HOD/LOD table — AUC 0.879 over 284 sessions — whose strongest call is the one nobody quotes: at the 20 % cell the extreme broke 85 % of the time (n=230, median 08:40 CT) against a ~57 % base. Joining that prior to this ladder is the next question, and it is asked after this is built, on data none of the analysis so far has seen.
