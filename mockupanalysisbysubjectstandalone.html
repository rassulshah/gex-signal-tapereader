The Analysis tab, by subject

Seven subjects, split the way a trader thinks about them, 170 studies. Every study is a question whose answer changes an action at the tap — size, side, target, stop, skip, time, level, wait — and nothing is on the tab for the sake of being known. This page is rendered from learning/studies.json, the one registry the tab, the Testing tab and the nightly will read.

Real numbers

Every number here is measured — there are no illustrative cells on this page. The 284-session ES corpus (0.879, NOT-IN 85% n=230, the range and clock base rates), the 11-day book corpus (94 episodes; tap decay 73% n=22 vs 47% n=70; H3 and H4 nulls), and the sweep study run today (919 sweep events over 284 sessions, 34 cells). A study with no number says thin, open or read next. Counts: 93 open · 22 thin · 17 read next · 15 read · 13 shipped · 4 registered · 3 refused · 2 draft · 1 blocked.

How to read a row: id · the question · DECIDES what it changes at the tap · the doctrine claim it tests (C-n, or lore for trader folklore, ours for our own) · the corpus it needs · its status. A second line carries the result, always with its n. tap record means the v15.55 TAP record (not yet shipped); API backfill means Skylit's historical heatmap (his decision, ~15–20k credits).

What the sweep study saysThe level's name does not matter. A bounce off the overnight low, the prior-day low or the IB low is the day's low exactly as often as a bounce off any fresh low at that minute (29% vs 28%, n=113; 23% vs 24%, n=87; 20% vs 26%, n=143). What matters is when (first 30 minutes: 27% vs 18%, n=180), how deep (>8 pts: 40% vs 24%, n=86) and how slow the reclaim (6–30 bars: 40% vs 24%, n=90). A shallow quick poke is not the low 86% of the time (n=228).
What it means for the tradeDo not buy the first reclaim of a level because it is the ONL; buy it because it flushed deep, took its time, and came early — and, the whole point of this application, because a top-5 node or the King sits there. That last clause is unmeasured: it is H6, the register entry the tap record exists to answer. When the sweep is the extreme, the far extreme is a median 60–89 pts away.
