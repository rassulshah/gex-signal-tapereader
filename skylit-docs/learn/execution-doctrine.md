# Execution Doctrine (skylit.ai/learn/execution-doctrine) — captured 2026-08-13

## One Rule
See setup on map -> price approaches node -> enter at the DIRECT TAP -> deflection plays out.
Entering anywhere but the direct tap = trading noise (no catalyst).

## Deflection Zones (tolerance band = valid tap window)
- QQQ & SPY: ± $0.50 around the node strike
- SPX:       ± 5 points around the node strike
Move THROUGH the zone and continue = overshoot = Beach Ball territory, reassess.

## Tap Probability (THE most important number)
- 1st tap of major node: ~80% reaction
- 2nd tap:               ~66%
- 3rd tap:               ~33%
- 4th+:                  spent (test of whether anything remains)
Drop 1->2 significant, 2->3 severe (non-linear). SIZE DOWN as tap count rises. 3rd tap needs exceptional confluence or skip.

## Regime-Based Execution
- POSITIVE gamma: hedges contrarian, mean-revert. FADE at nodes. Wider stops, tighter targets. Book at next node.
- NEGATIVE gamma: hedges pro-cyclical, momentum. FOLLOW. Tighter stops (fast fails), wider targets (runs further).

## Risk Management
- Minimum 3:1 R:R on every trade. Non-negotiable. If entry->target isn't 3x entry->invalidation, pass.
- Stop = ONE NODE BEYOND the invalidation level (not tight against entry). Stops vary with map spacing.
  Logic: if traded node holds, price won't reach next node; if it reaches next node, thesis failed.

## A+ Setup (all three required, else it's B-grade)
1. Chart structure (form thesis on chart FIRST — trend/S-R/prior H-L/volume).
2. Heatseeker confluence (FRESH, high-magnitude node at that chart level; check tap count).
3. Asymmetric R:R with proper execution (tap entry, correct stop, >=3:1, regime-matched management).
Fresh king node at chart support = A+. Thin gatekeeper w/ 2 prior taps at marginal level = not.
