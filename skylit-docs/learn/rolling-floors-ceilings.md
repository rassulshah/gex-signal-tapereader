# Rolling Floors & Ceilings (skylit.ai/learn/rolling-floors-ceilings) — captured 2026-08-13

## What Rolling Means
Floors/ceilings MOVE across updates. Watch the map across 2-3 consecutive updates, not one snapshot.
- Largest FLOOR node migrating HIGHER strike-by-strike over 2-3 updates = floor ROLLING UP (bullish).
- Largest CEILING node migrating LOWER = ceiling ROLLING DOWN (bearish).
The RANGE itself is shifting, not just price inside it.

## Rolling Floor Up (bullish) — 3 things to watch
- Dominant floor node at same time of day is 1-2 STRIKES HIGHER today vs yesterday.
- Adjacent nodes below the floor weakening/disappearing as exposure migrates up.
- Migration GRADUAL & CONSISTENT across updates — NOT a single-session spike.

## Rolling Ceiling Down (bearish)
- Dominant ceiling node at LOWER strike today vs yesterday & 2 days ago.
- Overhead nodes growing as positioning shifts lower.
- Confirmed when ceiling-to-spot distance narrows across multiple sessions.

## Rolling vs Breakout (IMPORTANT distinction)
Rolling = gradual sustained repositioning (floor keeps WORKING, dip-buy thesis intact).
Breakout = sudden structural event that OVERWHELMS structure (floor is GONE). Trade them differently.

## Identifying in Real Time (the count rule)
Pull last 2-3 map updates; find largest floor & largest ceiling in each; check migration.
- ONE migration over one update = NOISE.
- TWO consecutive same-direction migrations = SIGNAL.
- THREE = CONFIRMATION.
Speed matters: 2-strike jump in one update != 1 strike/session over a week — faster = more urgency.
Old vacated floor strikes leave AIR POCKETS behind — don't use an old floor as support after it rolled above.
