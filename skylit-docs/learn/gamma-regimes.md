# Gamma Regimes (skylit.ai/learn/gamma-regimes) — captured 2026-08-13
Regimes classify by DEALER POSITIONING (+/- gamma), not price direction. Three day types.

## Type 1: RANGE DAY
Signature: POSITIVE gamma predominates. Price stays inside a floor & ceiling. Trinity maps show NO confluence.
Dealers net long, lean against moves -> compression / mean-reversion.
Tactic: FADE THE EXTREMES. Sell the ceiling test, buy the floor test. Take profit BEFORE the midpoint. Wider stops, tighter targets.

## Type 2: TREND DAY
Signature: NEGATIVE gamma with RAPID ACCUMULATION. Spot sits on a key level; nodes far from current price.
Velocity maps show one-directional positioning. KING NODE GROWING RAPIDLY (not static). FLOORS/CEILINGS ROLLING.
Dealers net short, hedging pro-cyclical -> adds fuel to the move.
Tactic: FOLLOW direction, do NOT fade. Trailing stops behind the rolling floor/ceiling. Tighter stops, wider targets.

## Type 3: WHIPSAW DAY
Signature: NEGATIVE gamma + AIR POCKETS + CONFLICTING signals. Maps disorganized. Trinity confluence ABSENT.
Violent two-sided moves, wicky candles, fake moves are the norm.
Tactic: fade EXTREME ends of range only (on the most conflicted index, with a FRESH node), or SIT OUT. Never trade the middle.
