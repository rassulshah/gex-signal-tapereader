# Air Pockets, Liquidity Vacuums & Velocity Mode (skylit.ai/learn/air-pockets-velocity) — captured 2026-08-13

## Air Pocket
Zone of LOW/weak GEX exposure between nodes. No major node, no meaningful hedging friction.
On entry: (1) movement FASTER, (2) reactions WEAKEN, (3) direction CLEANER.
⚠️ NOT a target — a PATHWAY. You trade THROUGH it, target = next structural node on the OTHER side. Do NOT target the midpoint.

## Liquidity Vacuum
EXTENDED air pocket — multiple consecutive low-GEX zones stacked. Effect COMPOUNDS (not additive). Price can GAP; levels don't work.
Identifiable BEFORE arrival: wide stretch of near-zero exposure with no significant nodes = plan for accelerated transit. Absence of bars IS the signal.

## Velocity Mode (RATE OF CHANGE)
Measures how FAST dealer positioning is shifting (NOT node size/sign). Tracks: nodes growing, shrinking, appearing, disappearing across recent updates.
= dealer URGENCY. Growing = accumulating obligations; shrinking = covering/closing.
Air pocket = SPACE. Rate of change = FUEL. Space w/o fuel = drift (slow, directionless). Space + fuel = acceleration.

## The dangerous combo (negative gamma)
NEG GAMMA + AIR POCKET + HIGH RATE OF CHANGE (one direction) = fastest/cleanest moves in framework. Near-vertical, minimal pullback, failed bounces.
Sequence: cross polarity/break node -> hedging pro-cyclical -> enter pocket (no friction) -> rate-of-change accelerates -> near-vertical -> stops only at next node.
Wrong side: "price isn't stopping", "levels aren't working", "everything getting run through" = you're in an air pocket + high velocity, likely -gamma. GET OUT OF THE WAY.

## Pre-move velocity checklist (for OUR tool)
1. Air pocket adjacent to spot? 2. Rate of change elevated? 3. Gamma negative at spot? 4. Trigger (node test / polarity approach / catalyst)?
First 3 + a trigger = "loaded". Stops must be structural (next node), not dollar amounts.
