# Reading Heatseeker (skylit.ai/learn/reading-heatseeker) — captured 2026-08-13

## Node Hierarchy — 5 node types
1. KING NODE — strike with the LARGEST ABSOLUTE exposure (not largest +, not largest −; largest |v|).
   Center of structural gravity. ONE per session. Pins happen here at close (dealers flatten biggest book).
   When King moves, exposure picture rotated — pay attention.
2. FLOOR — largest exposure node BELOW spot. Dealers buy on approach. Strongest floor = BIGGEST below spot, NOT nearest.
3. CEILING — largest exposure node ABOVE spot. Dealers sell on approach. Strongest = biggest above spot.
   Floors/ceilings are NOT static — profile shifts intraday (see node-lifecycle).
4. GATEKEEPER — node sitting BETWEEN two larger structural nodes. Not the target; a CHECKPOINT.
   Holds -> price stalls in current region. Breaks -> path to next major node opens, price accelerates.
   Works both directions. Count gatekeepers between spot and King = friction estimate. Dense stack = lots of friction.
5. AIR POCKET — stretch of LOW/weak exposure. No floor/ceiling/gatekeeper. PATHWAY not target.
   ⚠️ Do NOT buy bottom / short top of an air pocket expecting a bounce. Trade the nodes on either side.
   Tells you the move will be FAST, not that it will stop. Big pocket below in decline / above in rally = speed coming.

## 5-Step Workflow (run in order, every session)
1. Identify the King Node (largest |exposure|) — structural anchor.
2. Mark floors & ceilings (largest below / largest above spot).
3. Locate Gatekeepers between spot and King — the checkpoints.
4. Spot air pockets — fast-travel zones.
5. Determine where spot sits relative to all of them — that context makes every read actionable.

## Never Trade the Midpoint
Midpoint between two nodes = NO structural significance, no dealer obligation, no gamma inflection.
"Support/resistance halfway between two levels is imaginary." Mark the nodes, trade the nodes.
Only treat the between-space as a PATHWAY to gauge speed.
