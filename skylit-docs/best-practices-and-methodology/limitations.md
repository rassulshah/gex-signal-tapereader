## Limitations

While **Heatseeker™** provides a powerful view into **dealer positioning**, it’s not a crystal ball (although many still believe it to be).\
It shows how **Market Makers (MMs)** are *currently* exposed to the market, not what will happen next.\
Understanding its boundaries helps you use the tool *as intended* and avoid false confidence.

### 1. Heatseeker™ Is Not 100%

* Heatseeker™ displays **dealer exposure**, not guaranteed future outcomes.
* It helps you **anticipate probability zones**.
* **Random events**, such as news headlines, earnings reports, or geopolitical shocks, can instantly alter dealer flows.

> Treat Heatseeker™ as a **map of influence**, not a prophecy of price.

### 2. Market Conditions Can Affect Accuracy

Certain conditions reduce the clarity and reliability of dealer data:

* **Low-volume periods** (like lunch hours) can distort map strength.
* **Illiquid tickers** or small caps often show *noise* instead of meaningful structure.
* Tickers that are set up for short squeezes can result in blowing past nodes.
* During **OPEX weeks** or **major rebalancing**, nodes may lose short-term influence as contracts roll off.

> If liquidity dries up, **price moves first**, and **maps catch up** later.

### 3. Map Reshuffles Don’t Always Favor You

* Reshuffles happen when **dealer exposure shifts quickly,** often without warning.
* Nodes can **vanish**, **weaken**, or **reappear** at new levels intraday.
* Even a valid thesis can fail if the reshuffle changes the structure underneath your trade.

> Focus on **risk-to-reward**, not just “being right.”\
> A strong R:R setup can remain profitable even if only **1 out of 5** plays succeed.

### 4. Context Matters More Than a Single Signal

* Don’t trade off a **single node** or **isolated event**.
* Always pair Heatseeker™ insights with:
  * **Price action**
  * **Liquidity context**
  * **Market structure** (higher timeframe)
* The strongest setups come when all three align.

> Heatseeker™ amplifies **contextual awareness**, not signal precision.
