The rule I will implement, in your words: a level qualifies only if it sits within a tolerance of the HIGH or the LOW — the edges. Anything price traded through and kept going is excluded by construction, because it is nowhere near either wick tip. IB and IB60 are excluded by name, so they can never appear even when they coincide.

Tolerance = 1 ATR, the same band the deflection work settled on and calibrated against your own circled charts. It scales with the day, so a level 0.4pts off a wick counts on a quiet morning and does not on a fast open.

D vs E is the whole question: 104px or 80px. D draws a hairline from the wick to the name, so you can see WHICH price the level sits at. E just stacks the names by the tip and lets position imply it. D is unambiguous when two levels are close together — which is exactly today, PDH and CW0 within a few ticks. I would take D; the 24px buys the one thing the label is for.
