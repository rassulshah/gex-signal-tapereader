why this reads better than rows of extremes

Every row is a leg, and each starts where the last ended — open→high→back to open→low→PT→close. Read top to bottom and you have walked the session. The old 1ST/2ND rows carried the same numbers but made you assemble the order yourself.

MOVE sums to the BODY. +3.57 −3.57 −3.43 +1.60 −0.60 = −2.43, which is exactly O→C. That is not decoration — it is the check that says no leg is missing. If the column stops summing, a leg has been dropped or double-counted.

TRAVEL and efficiency fall out for free. Price walked 12.77pts to produce a 7.0pt bar — 55% efficient. A trend day runs 80%+, a chop day 30%. That single number says what kind of day it was, and nothing on the face says it today.

The one I would drop: leg ② (the fold) is the only row that is not an extreme, and on many days it never happens. It carries BOP/W.END, which you asked for — so it stays as a row, but greyed, because it is a return, not a new high or low. Tell me if you would rather it fold into row ① as two extra columns.
