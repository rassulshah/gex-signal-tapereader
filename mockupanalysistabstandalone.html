Reading it top to bottom is reading your session. ① is what you know at 08:25. ② appears only while a node is being tested and is the one card the dashboard's callout line summarises — same function, one source. ③ is the journal the doctrine tells you to keep, with the one column that makes your two objectives one table: extreme?

Two things are carried on every number: its n with a Wilson interval, and which corpus it came from — price only (the 284 sessions) or with the book (the sessions since the recorder). And Skylit's own figure sits beside ours wherever the doctrine gives one, so the disagreement on tap decay (66% vs our 73%) is visible rather than buried.

What is honest about the empty state

Most "we measure" cells will read thin for a while — the tap record starts tomorrow and the conditions multiply fast. The card never invents a number for a thin cell; it shows Skylit's expectation alone and says so. The historical API backfill (Part 2 of the design document) is what fills the right-hand column in an afternoon instead of a season.
