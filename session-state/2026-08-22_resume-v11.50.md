### THE EM BAND (v11.49) — read this before touching it
- **`dte0.em` ONLY. A fallback to `toFri.em` is FORBIDDEN** and `test_em_band.js` fails the build on one.
  `toFri.em` is a single LATER expiry, not a five-day move.
- **Captured ONCE per day per symbol** into `gpts_emopen_v1`, keyed by `ctTodayStr()`, **never overwritten**.
  Overwrite it and the anchored band silently becomes a live one that follows price — the exact thing it
  exists not to be. A test pins this.
- **The open is `closedCandles(sym)[0].o`** — already filtered to today from 08:30 CT, so a noon start still
  measures from the real open.
- **`~EST`** = the EM was first captured more than `EM_FRESH_MIN` (15) minutes after the open, so the
  straddle had decayed and the band is NARROWER than the open's was. **No √T reconstruction** — it assumes
  IV has not moved, and on the days that matter it has.
- **SCALES:** `em` is SPX → chart via `dispScale`; candles are UNDERLYING → chart via `rr`. Both land on
  chart scale before anything is compared or drawn, and only then is `dispNum()` safe.
- **`emPos()` CLAMPS.** A mark outside the band pins to the rail; the percentage says how far past.
- The percentage is **displacement from the open ÷ EM** — user's choice over range-travelled, so the dot
  and the number are the same fact and cannot disagree.

**FLIP (was HVL).** HVL is SpotGamma's house term; the standard names are Zero Gamma / Gamma Flip and IF
itself says "Zero Gamma Level". Display strings and rank-map keys renamed; **`hvlSrc`, `LVL_COL.hvl`,
`P.hvl`, `isHVL`, `HVL_MAX_DIST` and recorded field names are deliberately UNCHANGED.**
