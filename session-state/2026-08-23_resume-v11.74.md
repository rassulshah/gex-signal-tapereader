### ⛔ THE TAPEREADER'S `ifFetch` MUST STAY OFF — I RECOMMENDED FLIPPING IT ON AND I WAS WRONG

declaration and it is why `IF_CFG` defaults `{on:false}`. The tapereader's `ifFetch`/`ifParse`/`ifLevels`
are DEAD CODE kept for reference. **The companion holds the grant; anything cross-origin belongs there.**
