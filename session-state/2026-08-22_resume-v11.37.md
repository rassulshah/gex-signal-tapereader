# RESUME NOTE — 2026-08-22 — v11.37 / companion v1.5

## ⚠ THE THREE THINGS THAT EXPLAIN MOST OF THIS PROJECT'S BUGS

**1. Mislabeling.** A number displayed under a label implying a different source, window or scale than
it actually had. The ladder said `IF` and rendered Skylit numbers. `week` said "to Fri" holding one
expiration. `DRIFT ✓` sat beside "both books not in yet". `FLRCEIL_FAR` was documented in STRIKES and
tested in PRICE POINTS. **Nothing ever throws.** Ask of every number: which book, which window, which
scale, and does the label say so.

**2. Moving denominators.** %King is right for ranking at one instant and WRONG for comparing two
moments — if the King strike changes, every node rebases at once. Anything measuring change over time
must use DOLLARS. This bit three separate features.

**3. Does it POINT, or does it CONDITION?** A direction vote answers "which way next". A conditional
quantity answers "what happens IF it moves". Gamma, vanna and VIX term structure all condition. Putting
them in a direction tally was the single biggest design error, and it survived several rounds because
each one *feels* directional.

## THE DESIGN PHILOSOPHY (user's, stated 2026-08-22)

> **"I am big on 50 sma, everything else is supplementary."**

BIAS is therefore **not a tally**. The 50-SMA gives the direction and explains itself. SKEW, ACCUM and
PA **confirm or they don't**, and the count is the confidence. Confirmers never outvote the SMA, and
three agreeing cannot manufacture a direction when the SMA is flat — both pinned by tests.

Why this is better: the old six-vote tally printed NEUTRAL on a 2–2 split while the SMA was plainly
sloped (the panel arguing with the chart), and TREND measured **34%** alone — so "TREND with 3 of 3
confirming" vs "0 of 3" is the more useful thing for the supplementary reads to tell you. That is also
testable via `dirFactorStats`, which computes lift against base rate with a regime split.

## VIX — RESEARCHED, AND IT FAILS THE DIRECTION TEST

- **Term structure** (VIX9D/VIX/VIX3M): *"primarily a regime filter, not a directional price signal."*
  `VIX9D > VIX` is the first reliable stress signal. → **FRAME, as a third regime axis. IF gives it to us
  as Term Slope, so we don't compute it.**
- **VIX change**: inverse of SPX by construction → tautological, same defect as ΔDEX. **Reject.**
- **VIX/price divergence**: *"a reversal warning and regime shift indicator, not a standalone directional
  signal"* — price at new highs while VIX ticks up = exhaustion. → **④ REACTION as a warning.** VIX is
  captured per bar already (`xm` in the day exports). **NOT YET BUILT.**

Sources: flashalpha.com/concepts/vix-term-structure · tradegex.pro/academy/vix-overlay-trading

## DEX vs GEX — what DEX is actually for
From the user's platform screenshots: **Net GEX profile runs in K–tens of M; Net DEX runs in hundreds of
M–BILLIONS** — ~100× larger, because delta is ~0.5 and gamma ~0.001.
- **GEX = the CHARACTER of movement** (pin vs expand). How price travels.
- **DEX = which way HEDGING PUSHES.** Dealers short delta must BUY as price rises (rallies get chased);
  long delta means they sell into strength.
- DEX is a supply/demand map, **not an arrow** → it belongs in FRAME as a number, not as a vote.
- Chain OI refreshes **once daily**, so intraday ΔDEX is mostly greeks re-weighting as spot moves — it
  would agree with price after the fact and look like a good vote while adding nothing.

## ROLL DETECTION — built (v11.34), SHADOW MODE
`rollDetect` reads `LASTFEED[sym].j.levels` — ~390 snapshots of `{k,v,net}` in DOLLARS, ~1min cadence,
whole session, arriving every fetch. `t` is in **SECONDS**. Window walked by TIMESTAMP, not index.

**Calibrated on real data**, not guessed: **30m · 50% RELATIVE TO THE SESSION MEDIAN · $40M · ±5 strikes**
→ 9 events/session. 35% gave 37 (noise), 70% gave 2 (silence). The median near-money strike **grows
10–15% per 30m** as the book builds; on expiry day strikes lost **75–94%** to decay alone. Without the
median subtraction the detector screams at both ends of every session.

**Direction unproven — do NOT make it a vote.** Ceiling-down 4 events/1 correct/mean **+0.39**;
floor-up 4/1/**−0.22**. Both leaned OPPOSITE to doctrine. And the archive cannot settle it: daily exports
keep only ~5 nodes/bar with `abs` often null and `hist` in %King; **all four archived days are DOWN days**
(net −3.11, −1.61, −2.24, −4.77) with a **66% base rate**, so any bearish signal inherits unearned edge
and the mirror case is untestable. The existing per-node `roll` flag **never once fires as
ceiling-rolled-down** in four sessions — it measures something other than its name.
