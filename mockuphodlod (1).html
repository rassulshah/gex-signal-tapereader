The 4-day sample was wrong about the thing you most wanted. It said 75% of days print the high first. Across 284 sessions it is 48% — a coin flip. Anyone who had built a bias on those four days would have been trading a pattern that does not exist.

What IS real, and it is better than the sequence would have been:
· 75% of sessions set one extreme by 10:30 ET, median 09:51 — about twenty minutes in. So on three days in four, by mid-morning one end of the day is already fixed.
· The other extreme prints at a median of 14:23, roughly four hours later. That gap is the ride.
· Median range is 56 pts (p25 42, p75 80) — that is the full distance between the two extremes.
· The survival curve is the edge. 30 minutes of standing means almost nothing (38%); two hours means 75%; three hours means 84%. It converts "the high was a while ago" into a number.

Lows hold slightly better than highs early on — at 15 minutes, 35% versus 26%. Small, but consistent with lows being made in panic and highs in drift.
