what changed, and the one thing to decide

Rows are now HIGH and LOW, not 1ST and 2ND — fixed, with ①/② badges for the order. The high row is always the high, so the two rows are true mirrors and the table never reorders itself mid-session.

OF BAR replaces WICK%, and now the column sums. 51% + 35% + 14% = 100% — upper wick, body, lower wick. That is the whole candle, and it is the check that says the decomposition is right.

A real finding, and it needs your call: the old PTWick% 22% is not the lower wick. It measures the PT retrace off the low (1.6pts); the lower wick is close-minus-low (1.0pt = 14%). Two different things, and only the second belongs in a column that has to sum to 100%. I have put 14% in the bar column and kept PT on the chart as the dashed violet mark, since the retrace is still worth seeing. If you would rather the column carry PT, it can — but then the three numbers stop adding up to the bar.

Still forming: C is "now", the body is drawn open-ended, and FOLD/SPAN/END on the LOW row are em-dash because price has not come back to the open. They will fill in if it does.
