lsGammaProfile 0.74: what the two fixes look like

Both rails as the plugin draws them with Book = Both, Medium thickness, font 10 — the SPY strip on the left (strike, ES price in the dimmer ink, %King), the SPX strip on the right. Values are illustrative, shaped like Friday's books.

1 · Hide nodes under %  — 0 (off) vs 20

Before 0.74 the setting only blanked the % text — the grey sub-20% bars, their strip rows and their badges all stayed. Now every node under the setting is gone on both rails: bar, strip row, badge, band. The Kings (SPY 761, SPX 7650) never hide. If a call or put wall sits on a hidden node, its line keeps the "CALL WALL" / "PUT WALL" label, since there is no node to carry the tag.

2 · Line style on the King lines  — King line width 2

The Line style row always applied to the King line — but Windows draws any line wider than 1 px as solid whatever its style, so at width 2 the King never changed while the 1-px CW / PW / FLIP lines did. That is why it looked like the style belonged to the other lines. 0.74 draws a wide dotted or dashed line as separate segments (dot: 2 on / 4 off, dash: 8 on / 4 off at width 2), so both King lines follow the style — and so does every other level line, as before.
