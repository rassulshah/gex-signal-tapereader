Rules

OrderFixed, from low to high in the book's logic, as today: PW · Mag · SPX King · SPY King · FLIP · CW, then EM H/L. An unticked item is skipped and the gap closes up.
Box widthThe chip keeps sizing itself to what it shows, so a short selection makes a small chip.
Regime lineUnticked, the chip is a single line of levels. If every box is unticked, no chip is drawn (same as unticking "Regime + read panel").
SPY KingOnly shown when Book = Both SPY and SPX, as today.
EM H/LNew: the expected-move edges as "EM low – high" in cyan, ES prices. Shown when the EM rows are in the file.
Depth pillsThe 0D / WK / MO badges beside PW and CW. Unticked, just the prices.
Also in this batch"King colour" is renamed King, so the colours line fits and "Polarity" is no longer cut off.
Nothing is built. It waits in the batch until you say "build".
