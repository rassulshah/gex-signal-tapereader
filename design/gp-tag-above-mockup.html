Rules the plugin would follow

1. Where. The tag's baseline is 1 px above the row's price text, left edge on the price's left edge. Rows are 26 px apart at font 10, so there is room; at font 8 the tag drops to 8 px.

2. One tag per row, in priority. K > CW / PW > MAG > G > C / F > B / P. A strike that is both the King and the call wall shows K·CW — the two joined with a middle dot, never two lines.

3. Colour is the level's, not the node's. CW red, PW green, FLIP cyan, MAG by its own polarity, K by the King's polarity, roles in plain grey. The price and % keep the polarity colour exactly as now.

4. FLIP is a price between strikes: the dashed tick across the strip stays, its tag moves from beside the bars to above the tick, inside the strip.

5. What goes away. The tags beyond the bar tips (CW / PW / MAG beside the tip), the pill beside the tip, and the line labels at Left / Center / Right — the level line still draws, its name is now on the strip. "Level labels at" becomes unused.

6. Both rails. The SPY strip gets the same treatment for its own tags (K, roles); IF levels stay on the SPX strip only, as today.
