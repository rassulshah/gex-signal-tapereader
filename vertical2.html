The expected sits to the right of each actual, dimmed and right-aligned, so every number is beside the thing it should be compared with. That is what the old E row did across the table; transposed, it becomes a third sub-column and reads better — you no longer have to track down two rows to find the pair.

A 1px rule between columns (#1e2733, the same tone as the section border) with 9px of air either side. Enough to separate, quiet enough not to read as a table grid.
