Two things the data changed in my design

Rolls are not conservation of mass. Receivers gained 2.8×, 8.6×, 13× and 16× what the losers lost. Several sources feed one destination. An equal-and-opposite test — which is what I would have written — would have detected almost nothing. The rule is direction + proximity + a magnitude floor, never a balance check.
Roll bias is its own signal. All five rolls point down and 7650 is receiving from two strikes at once. One line at the bottom says so, because five separate badges do not add up to "the book is migrating lower" in a trader's head at a glance.

Layout

Five numeric columns would not fit at 392px, so each node is two lines: identity on top (price · SPXW strike, %King, Size), the four horizons underneath on a fixed grid so they stay column-aligned down the list, then chips only when there is something to say.
