What replaced what

Gone: the 41/56/67/75/84 ladder (it answered the same question as the verdict, with a weaker instrument, and disagreed with it on the face) · the white honesty line at the bottom (its content moves into the header hover; only rates live stays visible) · “% of the range still ahead”, which printed even on days both extremes were already in.

New: one block that answers the question you actually put — where can the HOD get to, how likely, and when — in your own levels rather than in a price band. The 50% crossing of that column is the HOD estimate, so nothing was lost by dropping the band; it just carries a probability now instead of pretending to a number.

Not on the face and deliberately so: no entry, no stop, no target language. Every figure is descriptive and carries its n in the hover, and the whole block is labelled PROVISIONAL until it has been forward‑scored on live sessions.
