the answer Reading down is the process

  1. ① FRAME — regime, target, how much move is left
  2. ② BIAS — which side, and why
  3. ③ LOCATION — chart and ladder: are we anywhere worth trading
  4. ④ REACTION — is the level being defended
  5. ⑤ EXECUTE — entry, invalidation, target

The bar at the top shows where you are; the line under it says what you're waiting for. Steps below the current one stay dim until reached.

Defended above, thinning below. That's a short at the King with a target that should give way — and neither half of that read exists in either data source alone.

as agreed Green and purple both sides

Polarity colours the tape bars too, so purple means negative gamma wherever you look. Side is distinguished by anchor — chain grows from the left, tape from the right — rather than by hue.

check Does the bright edge read?

At 6px bar height the growth segment is small. It works on the King because the move is large; on a node that grew 5% it may be invisible. Might need a minimum visible width, or the marks alone carry it for small changes.
