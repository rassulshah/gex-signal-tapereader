⚠ THE WINDOW BUG IS THE ONE THAT MATTERS, AND IT HIDES INSIDE THE GROSS/NET BUG.

The band asks "how much room does TODAY have" and correctly reads the 0DTE straddle. The piles then answer "what is in the way" from a book that runs through next Friday — and because 2026-08-21 was a Friday, the roll means that window is today plus a whole extra week. Only 29.2% of the gamma being drawn on today's band expires today.

Watch what that does to a single strike:

Today's book is decisively short gamma at 7650. Next week's is long. Blend them and they cancel to a 1.2% residual — and the face then reports that residual as a $59M accelerator, because the size comes from gross while the direction comes from net. Two bugs producing one confident wrong number. Fix the window and the strike becomes honest; fix gross/net and the size becomes honest; fix only one and it is still wrong.
