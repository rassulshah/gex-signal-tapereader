new Rolls are an input, not a footnote

Five inputs, each with its own lean: trend, drift, rolls, path, Trinity. Today four point down and rolls point up — and rather than averaging that into a number, the line underneath names the disagreement: "the floor has rolled up 3×, the one input arguing against."

That's the honest read of today. Bearish tape, but dealers defending higher each time price came down. Exactly the setup where knowing the disagreement matters more than the verdict.

why this shape Disagreement beats averaging

A single blended score would have reported "weakly bearish" and hidden the tension. Showing the inputs separately means a 5/5 alignment and a 3/5 split look different at a glance — which is the distinction that should change your size, not just your direction.

unchanged Everything still scored

Rolls as a bias input is a new claim and gets a question of its own: does a counter-trend roll actually reduce the trend's hit rate, or is it noise? path_wall_roll and path_roll_age already record what's needed; this just puts the answer where you'll see it while it accumulates.
