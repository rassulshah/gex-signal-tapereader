Every badge hover — leading question, then the answer

badgehover
SKEWIs protection being bought or sold?Their published Δ Skew, read against its own recent range rather than as a level.
⚠ Index skew is permanently put-heavy, so the raw number would say the same thing every day.
ACCUMWhich side of the book is growing?Dollars added to calls vs puts over the last half hour. Growth is flow, and flow has a direction.
⚠ Measured in dollars, not %King — a percentage has a moving denominator and cannot compare two moments.
CROSSDoes the other index agree?QQQ's own trend, by the same rule on the same field as SPY's — 150 minutes of average, a 60-minute window.
⚠ The only confirm here that is not a second reading of this symbol's own option book.
⚠ Built from the 1-minute spot series, not candles: QQQ has none.
ROLLIs the settlement magnet moving?The King strike migrating up or down over the window — the board repositioning, not price.
⚠ Recorded since v11.0 and non-voting until v11.89. Whether it LEADS price is still the open question.
DRIFTIs anything structurally confirming the call?Gamma and vanna either lean the same way relative to price, or they split.
⚠ A GATE, not a vote — it is not in the count. Gamma says HOW price moves, not which way.
⚠ A ✓ means the books agree and point with the call. Agreeing with each other on the wrong side is an ✗.
the countHow much conviction is behind this call?How many live confirms agree with the 50-SMA that owns the direction.
⚠ Green only when every live one agrees; a dash means that read had nothing to give, which is not the same as disagreeing.
⚠ Recorded from v11.88 — TREND alone measured 34%, and whether the count improves on that has never been tested.

⚠ A new trend needs 15 of 20 bars; a reversal out of a broken trend needs 11.
⚠ FLAT means the SMA has no side — it is not assembled out of the confirms.
