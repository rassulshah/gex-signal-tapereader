3 · Regime badge hover

BEFORE
What kind of day is this? Gamma and vanna as a 2x2 — four cells, and you are in one of them: ▸ 1. −G −V the self-reinforcing cell. Dealers are short gamma AND falling spot pushes them to sell more, so hedging AMPLIFIES the move twice over. Breaks run, fades are the losing side, and normal stops are too tight. 2. −G +V dealers are short gamma, so they hedge WITH the move. Ranges expand and mean-reversion at levels is unreliable — breaks over fades. 3. +G −V gamma is pinning while vanna leans the other way. Levels hold until vol moves, then they give way quickly — fade the edges, but not with size. 4. +G +V dealers are long gamma and hedge AGAINST the move, which compresses range. Levels tend to hold and fades are the higher-probability trade. You are in cell 1. GAMMA = does dealer hedging fight the move or feed it. VANNA = does a change in vol add to that or work against it. Neither points anywhere — gamma tells you HOW price moves, not WHICH WAY — which is why they gate the call in ② BIAS rather than voting in it. It also sets what an extension means below: past 100% of the expected move reads STRETCHED only in cells 3 and 4, where range compresses. In cells 1 and 2 ranges expand, so running past the band is what a trend day does.
