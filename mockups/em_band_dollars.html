⚠ ONE THING I WILL NOT LET THIS BECOME. The standing rule is that this tool is descriptive: never entries, stops, sizing, R:R or P&L. A dollar figure is one step from all of those, so the framing matters. What is shown is the expected MOVE converted to dollars per contract — a unit change, nothing more. There is no position in it, no quantity, and no profit: "$200 used" means the day has travelled $200 worth of its expected move, not that anyone made or lost $200. The wording stays "per contract" and "of the expected move" for exactly that reason.

AND A HONEST CAVEAT ON WHICH CONTRACT. The multiplier follows the CHART, not what you trade. Chart ES while trading MES and every figure is 10× what it is worth to you. B's hover states the micro equivalent; D shows it inline. If you routinely chart one and trade the other, say so and the chip should show both rather than guess.
