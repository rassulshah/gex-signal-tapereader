WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT CLAIM.

Everything above is descriptive. Where the day has been, where it is, which regime it is in. Not one pixel says what happens next, and the words are chosen so none of them imply it — stretched, not reversing.

THE HYPOTHESIS, STATED SO IT CAN BE WRONG: past 100% of the expected move, price reverts toward the open MORE OFTEN in positive gamma than in negative gamma.

I cannot tell you whether that is true, and neither can this tool yet. Four archived day-files, 492 bars, effective n ≈ 49 independent observations — and every one of those four days is a DOWN day, so a directional claim drawn from them earns its accuracy for free. Worse: the band shipped today, so there are zero `emband` records in any of them. The hypothesis has never been tested once.

The scorecard must split by regime or it will measure nothing. Scoring reversion across both regimes together averages two opposite effects into mush and reports "no edge" — which would be the wrong conclusion drawn from the right data. Two questions, scored separately, non-voting until n≥20 each:
  · past 100% in +G — does price come back toward the open?
  · past 100% in −G — does it keep going?

And a third that answers your reversal question directly:
  · after a REVERSED shape appears, does the new direction hold, or does the day rotate again?

At roughly one session per weekday and only a handful of qualifying bars per session, n≥20 is weeks away. Until then these are reads on a face, not signals — and the tool should keep saying so.
