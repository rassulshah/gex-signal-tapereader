Rules 1–2 and 3–4 can never both fire: up ≥ 15 → dn ≤ 5, so the strict and loose paths are mutually exclusive by arithmetic, not by ordering. Reclaim needs 15 and falls out of rule 1 — no separate rule.

The cost, stated as a number rather than a worry.

Once a trend has been confirmed once, both directions flip at 11 — because after reversing to dn the prior becomes dn, and rule 4 then reverses back at up ≥ 11.

So the minimum distance between two opposite flips is:

 strict 15/15   dn=15,up=5  →  up=15   needs 10 new bars   30 min
 loose  15/11   dn=11,up=9  →  up=11   needs  2 new bars   6 min

In the worst composition the machine can flip direction every two bars. That is the price of the 12 minutes, and it lands in chop — exactly where a trend call does the most damage. Typical cases will be slower than the minimum, but the floor is 2 bars and it was 10.

If the recording shows this happening, the fix is a dwell time — a minimum number of bars in a state before a reversal may fire — not a return to 15. Not proposing it now; naming it so it is not rediscovered as a surprise.
