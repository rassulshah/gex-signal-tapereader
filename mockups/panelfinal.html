the point A question mark, not a flip

The verdict becomes ↓ BEARISH? in amber rather than flipping to bullish. Drift diverging doesn't mean up — it means the down call is unreliable. Halving your confidence and reversing your direction are very different responses, and the panel should say which one it means.

This is the only place the panel currently has measured evidence for anything directional, so it's the one state worth designing loudly.
