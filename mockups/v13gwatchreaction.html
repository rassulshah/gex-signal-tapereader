Reaction rebuilt on velocity — this is the real gain

It used to answer "is the level defending itself?" with wicks and VIX. Now the answer is in dollars: the node added +812K in 15 minutes while price sat on it. That is the level defending itself, measured, not inferred.

The verdict has three states, and they come from the tested node's own deltas:
· DEFENDING — adding while price tests it
· ABANDONING — bleeding while price tests it, which is the break tell
· NO REACTION — flat, and flat means no trade however good the location

The old signals stay as corroboration, ticked or crossed, so you can see that the wick agreed and VIX did not.

The honesty line

⚠ 60m still −1.4M — the defence is 15 minutes old, not an hour old. A node can be TURNING ↑ and still be down badly on the hour. Showing DEFENDING without that caveat would be the panel sounding more certain than the data.

Execute stays separate, and earns it here

REACTION says defending. ROLL BIAS says the book is migrating lower. Those genuinely conflict, and EXECUTE is where that gets resolved — half size, target the next node up rather than the EM high. Merged into one section, that conflict would have been a footnote under a green verdict instead of the thing that sized the trade.
