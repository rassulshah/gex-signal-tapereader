⚠ THE SANITY CHECK — THE BAND IS 25% TOO NARROW FOR WHAT IT CALLS ITSELF

The straddle extraction is correct. Our 34.65 reproduces InsiderFinance's own published ATM IV of 6.2% over the three calendar days to Monday's expiry, to within 0.03% — so the parsing and the strike selection are sound.

But the ATM straddle is not one standard deviation. It equals 0.8σ — the Brenner–Subrahmanyam identity — so:
 half-widthin σof sessions inside
what we draw now (raw straddle)34.650.80 σ57.6%
what "expected move" means43.311.00 σ68.3%

Every source agrees: expected move (1σ) ≈ ATM straddle × 1.25, and it is defined as the ~68% range. Our row is labelled OF EM while drawing a ~58% band. That is a value under a label that means something else — the same failure pattern as the week-vs-day EM, at a smaller scale.

Two honest fixes, and they are genuinely different products:
(a) multiply by 1.25 — the band becomes the real 1σ expected move, matches the label and every other platform, and gets 25% wider. A target at 87% of the rail today would sit at 80%.
(b) keep the raw straddle and rename the row STRADDLE — narrower, more conservative, and honest, but it is not what anyone else means by "expected move".

SECOND, SMALLER FINDING. On a weekend dte0 is Monday's expiry, so the straddle spans Friday close → Monday close and contains the weekend gap. That is why 34.65 looks large for one session. Captured at Monday's open the straddle covers only the remaining day and will be smaller — the band is right on a live day, and slightly wide in replay. Worth knowing before reading the replay band as if it were a session range.
