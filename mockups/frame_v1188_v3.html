① FRAME v11.88 — rev 3

Four things were wrong in rev 2.

1. The roles did not sit over their own nodes. KING was drawn at 93% while its chip is at 96% — I had clamped the label inward to keep it off the edge, which breaks the one thing a role label is for. Fixed properly: the anchor stays at the true position and near an edge the text extends inward from it (right-aligned) instead of being centred. Alignment is now exact everywhere.

2. T and GK are the same level — the target IS the heaviest strike, by construction. Two marks stacked on one price. They merge into GK · T.

3. SUCC was orphaned on its own line. It is a level, so it belongs on the rail — and since it sits below the band it gets an off-scale marker at the edge, which says "outside" honestly instead of pretending a position.

4. PAST ran off the right end. Same right-align rule.

The alignment rule

WRONG (rev 2) clamp the anchor: left:93% when the node is at 96%
the label no longer sits over the thing it names

RIGHT anchor stays at the node: left:96%
centred normally: translateX(-50%)
within ~6% of an end: translateX(-100%) (or 0% at the left end)
so the text grows INWARD from the node it belongs to

same rule for the role tier, the level text, the money figures and the price pill.
one rule, four users — nothing gets its own special case.

T merges when |target − node| rounds to the same ES point -> "GK · T"
off-band level: ◀ / ▶ marker pinned at the rail end, never a faked position
