How the four figures stay honest

a segment narrower than 9% keeps its number — it is pushed to the nearer edge of its
own span instead of being centred, and dropped ONLY if it would overlap a neighbour
already drawn. A dropped figure is then named in the hover, never silently gone.

a NEGATIVE remaining renders the word PAST in red, not a minus sign — the day has
spent the whole side and gone through it, which is a state, not a quantity.

hover on any of the four: "How much of this side is spent?"
